About LIYC

Harmonizing Talent & Community

Founded in 2023, the Long Island Youth Chamber (LIYC) provides a vibrant platform for gifted young instrumentalists to collaborate, refine their artistry, and share the profound beauty of classical chamber music with audiences across Long Island.

Chamber Mastery

Intimate string and harp ensemble coaching focusing on deep listening, phrasing, stylistic precision, and profound musical communication without a conductor.

Teen Mentorship

Designed specifically for talented teens ages 13-18. We foster peer leadership, confidence, and artistic growth in a welcoming, inspiring environment.

Community Outreach

Bringing classical masterpieces to senior residences, parish soirees, and community centers, spreading joy and connection throughout Long Island.

Mentorship & Growth

LIYC Apprenticeship Program

Are you an ambitious teen musician (ages 13–18) playing violin, viola, cello, double bass, or harp? Join our prestigious apprenticeship program to receive expert chamber coaching, collaborate with dedicated peers, and perform in prestigious community venues across Long Island.

Weekly Chamber Coachings

Work closely with distinguished teaching artists and seasoned student leaders.

Performance Opportunities

Perform in seasonal concerts, senior soirées, and community outreach events.

Leadership & Mentorship

Develop artistic independence, ensemble listening, and lifelong friendships.

Audition & Eligibility Requirements

  • Age Range: Open to talented musicians ages 13 to 18.
  • Instrument: Violin, Viola, Cello, Double Bass, or Harp.
  • Audition Format: Video submission of one classical movement or concerto piece.
  • Commitment: Weekly Sunday rehearsals on Long Island and scheduled concert dates.

Program & Repertoire

Notre Dame Parish's Senior Soiree

Wednesday, September 23, 2026 • 4:00 PM • 5 Mayfair Rd, New Hyde Park, NY

1. Mozart: Quartet No. 14 in G Major, K

A breathtaking early classical masterpiece showcasing delicate interplay between violins.

2. Grétry: Tambourin (ballet suite Céphale et Procris)

A lively, whimsical French baroque dance movement full of charm and verve.

3. Bach: Violin Concerto in D Minor for Two Violins

Exquisite contrapuntal dialogue performed by our stellar teen soloists.

4. Handel: Harp Concerto in B flat major & Hasselmans: La source, Op. 44

Ethereal harp solos capturing the cascading sparkle of flowing brooks and courtly elegance.

5. Massenet: Thaïs - Méditation & Bach: Partita in D Minor

Profound meditative beauty and soaring lyrical violin melodies.
